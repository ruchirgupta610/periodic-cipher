3-Digit Cipher Specification Reference

001 – 118: Chemical Elements

Atomic numbers formatted to 3 digits. Matches all 118 official elements (e.g. 001=H, 006=C, 044=Ru, 077=Ir, 118=Og)[cite: 8].

201 – 212: Fallback Alphabet

Covers the 12 English letters with no single-letter elemental symbol: A(201), D(202), E(203), G(204), J(205), L(206), M(207), Q(208), R(209), T(210), X(211), Z(212)[cite: 8].

000: Word Delimiter & Punctuation

000 encodes spaces cleanly without breaking the 3-digit fixed-width segmentation rule[cite: 8]. Non-alphabet chars remain clean[cite: 8].
